**Economic Indicators:**
- Malaysian GDP growth rates and sector contributions
- Inflation trends and BNM policy responses
- Government fiscal policy and budget allocations
- Export/import data affecting listed companies

**Regulatory Environment:**
- Bursa Malaysia listing requirements and compliance
- Securities Commission Malaysia guidelines
- Foreign investment regulations and limits
- Corporate governance requirements

**Market Structure:**
- Main Market vs ACE Market dynamics
- Board lot trading system (100 shares minimum)
- Settlement cycle (T+2) considerations
- Trading hours: 9:00 AM - 12:30 PM, 2:30 PM - 5:00 PM (GMT+8)

**Sector Considerations:**
- Plantation sector (palm oil price sensitivity)
- Banking sector (BNM regulation, interest rate sensitivity)
- Technology sector (government digitalization initiatives)
- Healthcare sector (aging population, medical tourism)
- Industrial sector (infrastructure development, export manufacturing)

**Currency and External Factors:**
- MYR strength/weakness vs USD, SGD, CNY
- Commodity price impacts (palm oil, rubber, tin)
- Regional ASEAN economic integration effects
- China economic relationship and trade impacts

### Risk Management in Malaysian Context:

**Liquidity Considerations:**
- Minimum daily volume requirements for micro-caps
- Market maker presence and bid-ask spreads
- Exit strategy planning for thinly traded stocks

**Regulatory Risks:**
- Disclosure requirements at 5% ownership threshold
- Related party transaction restrictions
- Foreign ownership limit compliance

**Market-Specific Risks:**
- Malaysian market correlation with regional markets
- Currency devaluation impacts on import-dependent companies
- Government policy changes affecting specific sectors
